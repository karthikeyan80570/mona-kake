Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about placing your pre-order.

How do I order without an online cart?
+
It's super simple! Just browse our menu above, pick the treat and size you want, and click "Order on WhatsApp". This directly opens WhatsApp on your phone or computer with your selection pre-filled. You can chat directly with the baker, share your address, and confirm!
Why are orders accepted only as pre-orders?
+
To guarantee maximum freshness! We are a boutique home kitchen, not a commercial factory. We bake every item fresh once your order is confirmed, allowing 1 day for proper cooling, setting, and decoration.
Where in Chennai do you deliver?
+
We deliver across major neighborhoods in Chennai. Delivery charges depend on the distance from our kitchen. You can share your pincode or location in WhatsApp for the exact delivery estimate.
Can I customize toppings, designs, and messages?
+
Absolutely! Our brownie slabs and custom celebration cakes can be customized with personalized birthday greetings, chocolate toppings, roasted nuts, or festive themes. Just mention your preferences in the WhatsApp chat.
Are eggless cakes available?
+
Yes, eggless options are available for many of our brownies, jar cakes, and celebration cakes upon request. Please let us know when ordering on WhatsApp.
